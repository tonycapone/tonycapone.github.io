Hermes personal

Hermes personal is a private assistant that Tony Howell runs on his own computer, for his own use. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it.

With Tony's permission, it can read his own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive (read only) to answer his questions. It cannot send, create, change, share or delete anything.
