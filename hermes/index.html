Hermes personal

Hermes personal is a private assistant that Tony Howell runs on his own computer, for his own use. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it.

With Tony's permission, it can read his own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive to answer his questions, and create new Google Drive files for him. It cannot send email, change calendars, or touch any file it did not create.
